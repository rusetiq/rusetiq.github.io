## RPG Urban Pack

- Creator: Kenney
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-urban-pack
- Version: 1.0
- License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0
- Local file: `rpg-urban-atlas.png`

The game uses selected roads, paving, waterfront, trees, lamps, barriers, furniture, and city props from the atlas. Its heroine, Abu Dhabi landmarks, interface, mission art, and lily accessory are original game-specific pixel drawings.
